Who gets blocked automatically

  • Personal mailboxes (Gmail, Hotmail…): the law requires consent for these
  • Sole traders and partnerships, where known
  • Anyone who unsubscribed, bounced or objected
  • Dissolved or insolvent companies

Mailboxes

Good practice

  • Send from a separate domain (e.g. getsitesentinel.co.uk), never your main one
  • Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC for that domain before sending
  • Keep 30–50 emails a day per mailbox. Add mailboxes to grow, don't raise caps
  • Watch the bounce rate: pause if it goes over 3%
